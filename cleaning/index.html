✦ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers before you book.

The questions we hear most from new clients across the GTA.

Will I have the same cleaner every visit?

Yes. We assign one dedicated cleaner to your home rather than a rotating team. Once your regular service is set up, the same person returns and gets to know your home and preferences.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients arrange access through a neighbour, building management, or another secure method. Some allow us to hold a key, stored securely and identified only by a reference number.

Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. All cleaners are screened, trained, and employed directly by us — not contractors. They're bonded and covered by liability insurance and WSIB.

What if I'm not happy with a clean?

Tell us within 48 hours and we'll return to fix it, promptly and at no extra charge — our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
