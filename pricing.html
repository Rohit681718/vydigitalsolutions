Pricing FAQ

Common questions about our pricing.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Government filing fees are separate and disclosed upfront — our service fee is always quoted before any work begins.

What determines my bookkeeping rate?

Monthly transaction volume and account complexity. Most sole proprietors and small corporations fall in the $100–$250/month range.

Can I bundle incorporation and bookkeeping?

Yes — many clients incorporate and set up ongoing bookkeeping together. Ask about bundled onboarding pricing during your consultation.

Do you offer a payment plan?

Yes, monthly services are billed monthly. Incorporation is a one-time fee, payable when we file your articles.
