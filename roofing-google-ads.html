There's a Real Difference Between Searching and Scrolling

Google Search Ads reach people in the exact moment they're looking for a roofer — a fundamentally different kind of attention than a social ad someone scrolls past.

🔍 Active Search

Someone typed "roofer near me" right now. High intent, ready to compare and call — you're answering a question they're already asking.

📱 Passive Advertising

Someone scrolling social media may not need a roofer at all right now. Lower intent, awareness-building only.

🎯 What We Target

"Roofer near me," "roof repair," "roof replacement cost" — high-intent searches, not broad unqualified traffic.
